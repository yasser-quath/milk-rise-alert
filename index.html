How to use it

  1. Place the phone above the pot but tilt it enough that the milk surface edge is visible, not perfectly flat from straight overhead.
  2. Tap Start camera and wait for the preview.
  3. When the milk looks calm, tap Set baseline.
  4. Tap Start monitoring. The alarm fires after several rising frames in a row.
